## Failure causes and residuals

- The 2-hour executor limit stopped the run. Serial local-model latency (screen about 500 s per
  entity, verifier timeouts at 900 s) is the bottleneck. A full run needs a supervised lane
  with no 2-hour limit, or a shorter `--timeout`.
- Crawl coverage is 4 of 18 entities (22 %). Cause unproven (see the steps table). The driver
  now writes `steps.jsonl` after each step, so a stopped run keeps its step table.
- Search results were saved without filtering, so some of the 18 entities are probably not
  providers (directories, articles). Screening only reached entities that had a snapshot.
- 2 verifier timeouts (900 s), as in LM-04 (assess_verify latency, cause unproven).
- No engine defect found. No engine or rubric change.
