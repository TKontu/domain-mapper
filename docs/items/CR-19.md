# CR-19: robots.txt status handling per RFC 9309 §2.3.1

Status and dependencies live in the canonical backlog, not duplicated here.

- Target references: docs/runs/M1-01.md "Robots diagnosis (round 195/A1)" (most M1-01 hosts answer
  robots.txt with a 3xx that the engine treated as disallow-all); `RobotsPolicy._load` in
  `adapters/crawl_policy.py`; `get_robots` in `adapters/http_transport.py`; D-003.
- Objective: follow robots.txt redirects (301/302/303/307/308) for at most 5 hops and parse the
  final 2xx body; more hops, a loop, a missing/invalid `Location` or a non-http(s) target is
  unreachable (disallow-all). Relative `Location` resolves against the current robots URL. Rules
  from the final body apply to the original origin (cache key unchanged). Any 4xx after redirects
  (incl. 401/403/404/410) is allow-all; 5xx or a transport exception stays disallow-all.
  `Crawl-delay` and `Disallow` parsing are unchanged.
- Design: `get_robots` returns `(status, body, location)` (Location only on 3xx) and still never
  follows redirects itself; `RobotsPolicy` follows hop by hop, so every hop keeps the byte cap,
  timeout and (in the CLI) the per-host limiter wait. The policy also accepts the old two-tuple
  `(status, body)` from injected fakes. Page-fetch redirect handling is unchanged.
  `adapters/fake_firecrawl_server.py` gains `robots_redirect` (`301`, `302`, `loop`).
- Scope / non-goals: no 5xx retry, no live network, no M1 rerun, no page redirect change.
- Acceptance: tests in `tests/test_crawl_policy.py` (redirect statuses, chain of 5, 6 hops, loop,
  relative Location, invalid Location, 4xx allow-all incl. after redirect, 5xx/exception after
  redirect, Crawl-delay and Disallow from the redirected body), `tests/test_http_transport.py`
  (Location surfaced, not followed), `tests/test_fake_firecrawl_server.py` (redirect modes) and
  `tests/test_cli_crawl_robots_redirect.py` (CLI crawl through the fake backend with a 301/302
  robots crawls pages; a loop skips them; red at base).
- Evidence / residuals: executor round 196/A1, offline only. Residuals: no 5xx retry; the
  diagnosis script `scripts/robots_diagnose.py` still reports the raw first-hop status (it drops
  the Location); effect on real hosts needs an M1 rerun.
