# AG-01: topic scores and coverage

Status and dependencies live in the canonical backlog, not duplicated here.

- Target references: ARCHITECTURE.md §10 Aggregate & score (topic score = mean of mapped status
  values over applicable, non-silent criteria; coverage = share of applicable criteria with
  evidence; both shown together); §5 data model; `core/scope_pack.py` (`Rubric`, `Topic.segments`,
  `Criterion.applies_to`, `Status.value`); `core/assessment_store.py` (`CurrentAssessment`,
  `CurrentEvidence.grounded`); D-003 (neutral fixtures).
- Objective: new pure module `core/aggregate.py` with frozen dataclass `TopicScore(topic_id: str,
  score: float | None, coverage: float | None, applicable: int, scored: int, evidenced: int)` and
  `score_topics(rubric: Rubric, assessments: Iterable[CurrentAssessment], entity_segments:
  Collection[str]) -> list[TopicScore]`, returning one `TopicScore` per rubric topic in rubric
  order. A criterion is applicable when its topic applies to the entity (empty topic segments
  mean all segments), its `applies_to` intersects `entity_segments`, and its assessment is not an
  explicit "does not apply" (status id `na` with a null scale value). `scored` counts applicable
  criteria whose status maps to a non-null value and `score` is their mean (`None` when none);
  `silent` and any other null-valued status that is not `na` stay applicable but are excluded
  from the score. `evidenced` counts applicable criteria whose assessment has at least one
  grounded evidence item and `coverage = evidenced / applicable` (`None` when no criterion is
  applicable). Applicable criteria without an assessment count in `applicable` only. Assessments
  for criterion ids outside the rubric are ignored; a duplicate criterion id or a status id
  outside `rubric.status_scale` raises `ValueError` (the caller filters to one rubric version).
- Scope / non-goals: `core/aggregate.py`, `tests/test_aggregate.py`, this file and the AG-01
  `todo.md` row. No database access, schema, CLI, profiles/weights, market statistics or silent
  penalty.
- Acceptance: offline pytest failing at base (module missing) and passing at head — mean of
  values; silent excluded from the score but counted in applicable and coverage; `na` removes a
  criterion; segment filtering at topic and criterion level; unassessed applicable criterion;
  ungrounded-only evidence not counted; zero-applicable topic gives `None` score and coverage;
  duplicate criterion id and unknown status raise `ValueError`; unknown criterion ignored; rubric
  topic order. The rubric is built from `load_scope_pack("scopes/_example").rubric` and/or inline
  neutral models. Full gate green.

  state for one entity (bounded by rubric size).
